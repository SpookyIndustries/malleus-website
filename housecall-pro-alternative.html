MALLEUS

Housecall Pro Alternative for Service Businesses

Malleus is a field service management platform for home-service and trade businesses looking for a Housecall Pro alternative with flat pricing and no per-seat costs.

Housecall Pro is popular with home-service businesses, but it prices by number of users and reserves some features for higher tiers. For a growing crew, that adds up.

What Malleus offers instead

  • No per-user fees, unlimited technicians at every tier.
  • Flat published pricing, no per-seat scaling.
  • All features included at every tier: scheduling, dispatching, estimates, invoicing, inventory, point of sale, marketing automation, and customer notifications.
  • Offline-first, so field work does not stop when the signal does.
  • Founding-customer access to the founder for setup and feature requests.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Housecall Pro alternative?

Malleus is a strong Housecall Pro alternative for service businesses that want flat pricing and no per-user fees. It covers scheduling, dispatching, estimates, invoicing, inventory, and point of sale, with every feature included at every tier.

Is Malleus cheaper than Housecall Pro?

Malleus uses flat published pricing with no per-user fees, so cost does not scale with team size. For shops with several technicians, that is usually the bigger saving over per-seat pricing.

Does Malleus include point of sale like Housecall Pro?

Yes. Malleus includes a retail point of sale alongside field service scheduling, dispatching, invoicing, and inventory, all at every tier.
