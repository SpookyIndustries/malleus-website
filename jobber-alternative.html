Jobber Alternative with No Per-User Fees

Malleus is a field service platform for service businesses that have outgrown Jobber's per-user pricing, or want to avoid it from the start.

Jobber is a solid tool for small home-service businesses, but its pricing is tiered by number of users and some features are reserved for higher plans. As your team grows, the per-user cost grows with it.

What Malleus does differently

  • No per-user fees. Add as many technicians as you want without changing your bill.
  • Every feature at every tier. Scheduling, dispatching, estimates, invoicing, inventory, marketing automation, and customer notifications are all included, not gated.
  • Flat, published pricing. You know the number up front.
  • Offline-first. Works without a signal in the field.
  • Multi-industry. The same platform runs field service, retail point of sale, and aviation MRO.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Jobber alternative for a growing team?

Malleus is a strong Jobber alternative for teams that do not want per-user pricing. It includes scheduling, dispatching, estimates, invoicing, inventory, and marketing automation at every tier, with no per-user fees, so cost does not rise as you add technicians.

Is there a field service app with no per-user fees?

Yes. Malleus charges no per-user fees. Every plan includes unlimited users, which is unusual in a market where most tools price per seat.

Does Malleus gate features behind higher tiers like Jobber?

No. All core features are included at every tier. Tiers differ by scope of setup and customization, not by which features you can use.
