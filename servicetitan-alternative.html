MALLEUS

ServiceTitan Alternative for Small and Midsize Contractors

Malleus is a field service management platform built for small and midsize contractors who find ServiceTitan too expensive and too complex for the size of their shop.

ServiceTitan is powerful, but it is built and priced for large enterprises: per-technician pricing, long annual contracts, and an implementation that can run months. For a shop running a handful of trucks, much of that is cost and complexity you never use.

Why contractors switch

  • No per-user fees. Unlimited technicians at every tier. Your bill does not climb every time you hire.
  • Published, flat pricing. A one-time deployment fee plus a flat monthly subscription. No per-seat math, no "contact sales."
  • All features at every tier. Nothing core is locked behind an upgrade.
  • 2 to 3 week deployment, not a multi-month rollout.
  • Works offline. Job data lives on the device, so techs keep working in basements, rural sites, and dead zones.
  • Direct founder access. Early customers get the founder on the phone, not a ticket queue.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ServiceTitan alternative for a small business?

Malleus is a strong ServiceTitan alternative for small and midsize contractors. It handles scheduling, dispatching, estimates, invoicing, and inventory with no per-user fees, flat published pricing, and a 2 to 3 week deployment, instead of ServiceTitan's enterprise pricing and multi-month onboarding.

Why do contractors look for a ServiceTitan alternative?

Usually cost and complexity. ServiceTitan uses per-technician pricing and long contracts and is built for large companies, so smaller shops often pay for capacity they do not use. Malleus offers flat pricing with unlimited users and a faster setup.

Does Malleus charge per user like ServiceTitan?

No. Malleus has no per-user fees. Every tier includes unlimited technicians.
